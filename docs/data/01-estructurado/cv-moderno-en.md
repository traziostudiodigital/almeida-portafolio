## Executive Summary

Specialist with more than 40 years of uninterrupted experience in the protection, registration, valuation, forensic examination, and appraisal of Cuban and Ibero-American cultural heritage, with over 20,000 works of art and heritage assets inventoried and appraised. Head of the Registration and Inventory Department at Cuba's National Registry of Cultural Property for 19 years (41-year total institutional tenure) and Head Technical Specialist in charge of Airport Customs and Cultural Property Seizures (1988–2024). Accredited partition appraiser (*perito tasador-partidor*) in more than 15 estate and inheritance liquidations. Associate Professor with over 25 years of active university teaching (University of the Arts, University of Havana, and San Gerónimo College). Co-author of academic publications for UNESCO (*Cultura y Desarrollo* Journal No. 10, 2013) and Ludwig Foundation / National Cultural Heritage Council books on art appraisal (2010). Official representative of Cuba at international heritage forums (UNESCO/OEI Cusco Forum) and diplomatic missions across more than 15 countries. Institutional collaborator with the Italian Carabinieri TPC and the IBERMUSEOS network. Awarded the Distinction for Cuban Culture (2006). <!-- Source: perfil.md; Respuestas.md -->
